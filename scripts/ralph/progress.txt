**Learnings for future iterations:**
- IBAN Mod-97 algorithm: Move first 4 chars to end, replace letters with numbers (A=10...Z=35), check mod 97 = 1
- IBAN lengths vary significantly by country: shortest is NO/NO at 15 chars, longest are LC/MU at 30-32 chars
- BBAN formats vary widely: some countries use only digits (DE, DK, NO), others use mixed alphanumeric (FR, GB, NL)
- Bank identifier positions within BBAN are not standardized: can be at start, middle, or vary by country banking system
- Some countries have special BBAN structures with check digits or control codes embedded (IT has check character at position 0)
- SEPA includes 36 countries: 27 EU members + EEA (NO, IS, LI) + CH, UK, MC, SM, VA, AD
- Major non-SEPA IBAN countries include Middle East (GCC countries), some Latin American countries (BR, CR, GT, DO), and various others
- IBAN Registry maintained by SWIFT is the authoritative source for country-specific IBAN formats
- Always validate example IBANs with Mod-97 to ensure correctness of check digits
